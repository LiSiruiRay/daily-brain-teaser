# The Harmonic Function That Knows Its Boundary

Let $u : \overline{\mathbb{D}} \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous on the closed unit disk $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$ and harmonic on the open disk $\mathbb{D}$. Suppose that $u$ vanishes on the **upper** semicircle $\{e^{i\theta} : 0 \leq \theta \leq \pi\}$ and is non-negative on the **lower** semicircle $\{e^{i\theta} : \pi \leq \theta \leq 2\pi\}$.

Must $u(0) \geq 0$?

Moreover, what is the **exact value** of $u(0)$ in terms of the boundary data?
