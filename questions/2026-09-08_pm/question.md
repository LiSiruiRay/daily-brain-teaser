# The Covering Space That Knows Its Sheets

Let $p: \widetilde{X} \to X$ be a covering map where $X$ is path-connected and locally path-connected. Suppose $\widetilde{X}$ is also path-connected.

**Prove** that the number of sheets (i.e., the cardinality of any fiber $p^{-1}(x)$) equals the index $[π_1(X,x) : p_* π_1(\widetilde{X}, \widetilde{x})]$.

In particular, deduce: if $X = S^1$ and $\widetilde{X} = S^1$ with $p(z) = z^n$ (the $n$-sheeted cover), what is the index, and which subgroup of $\mathbb{Z}$ does the covering correspond to?
