# The Sock Drawer Surprise

A drawer contains $r$ red socks and $b$ blue socks. You draw socks one at a time, **without replacement**, until you get a **matching pair** (two of the same color). 

What is the probability that the **first matching pair is red**?

(Assume $r \geq 2$ and $b \geq 1$.)

**Hint:** Think about what configuration of the first three draws forces a red match first.
