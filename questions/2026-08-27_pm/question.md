# The Entire Function Bounded on a Line

Let $f$ be an entire function such that $f$ is real-valued on the real axis and purely imaginary-valued on the imaginary axis. Show that $f$ is an odd function, i.e., $f(-z) = -f(z)$ for all $z \in \mathbb{C}$.
