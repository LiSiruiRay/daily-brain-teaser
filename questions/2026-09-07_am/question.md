# The Continuous Function That Must Have a Fixed Point

Let $f : [0,1] \to [0,1]$ be continuous. We all know by the Intermediate Value Theorem that $f$ must have a fixed point. Now consider the **circle**: let $g : S^1 \to S^1$ be continuous. Must $g$ have a fixed point?

If not, construct an explicit counterexample. If yes, prove it.

Now here is the real question: suppose $h : [0,1] \to [0,1]$ is continuous and **surjective**. Prove that $h$ must have **at least two** fixed points, or find a counterexample.
