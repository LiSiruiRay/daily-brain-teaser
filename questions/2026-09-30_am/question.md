# The Flippant Juror's Cousin: Four Jurors and a Coin

A four-person jury must reach a verdict by **majority vote** (at least 3 out of 4 agree). Three members each independently vote correctly with probability $p$, while the fourth member **flips a fair coin** to decide their vote.

A **three-person panel** (with no coin-flipper) also decides by majority (at least 2 out of 3), each member independently correct with probability $p$.

Which panel has a higher probability of reaching the correct verdict?
