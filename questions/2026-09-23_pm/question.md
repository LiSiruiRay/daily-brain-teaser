# The Ballot Box Twist: Runs of the Same Color

A bag contains $r$ red balls and $b$ blue balls, with $r > b$. They are drawn one at a time without replacement, in uniformly random order. What is the probability that the red balls are **never** in the minority throughout the drawing — meaning at every point in the sequence, the number of red balls drawn so far is **strictly greater** than the number of blue balls drawn so far?

*(Assume $r > b$ and count from the very first draw.)*
