# The Laurent Series That Refuses to Converge Everywhere

Let $f(z) = \dfrac{1}{z(z-1)}$.

**Part (a):** Find the Laurent series expansion of $f$ valid in the annulus $0 < |z| < 1$.

**Part (b):** Find the Laurent series expansion of $f$ valid in the region $|z| > 1$.

**Part (c):** Both series represent the same function $f$, yet they look completely different. What is the conceptual reason they must differ?
