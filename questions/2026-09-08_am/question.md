# The Quotient Space That Becomes a Cone

Let $X = [0,1] \times [0,1]$ be the unit square, and define an equivalence relation $\sim$ by collapsing the **entire top edge** to a single point:

$$(x, 1) \sim (x', 1) \quad \text{for all } x, x' \in [0,1].$$

No other identifications are made.

**Question:** What familiar topological space is $X/{\sim}$ homeomorphic to? Prove your claim.
