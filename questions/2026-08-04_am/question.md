# The Topological Group Whose Underlying Space Is a Sphere

Let $G$ be a topological group (a group with a topology making multiplication and inversion continuous). Suppose the underlying topological space of $G$ is homeomorphic to $S^n$ (the $n$-sphere).

For which values of $n \geq 0$ can this happen?

**Hint:** Think about what a topological group structure forces on the fundamental group and higher homotopy groups. Don't try to classify all possibilities — just find which small values of $n$ work and argue why large $n$ cannot.
