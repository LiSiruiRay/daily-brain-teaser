# The Quotient That Closes Up: $[0,1] \times [0,1]$ Mod Its Boundary

Consider the unit square $I^2 = [0,1]\times[0,1]$ with its usual topology. Let $\partial I^2$ denote its boundary (the four edges).

Define the quotient space $X = I^2 / \partial I^2$, where all points of $\partial I^2$ are identified to a single point $*$.

**Prove that $X$ is homeomorphic to $S^2$, the 2-sphere.**

*Hint: Think geometrically. Can you describe a continuous bijection explicitly?*
