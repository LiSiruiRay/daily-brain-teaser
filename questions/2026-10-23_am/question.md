# Four Random Points on a Sphere: Does the Tetrahedron Contain the Center?

Choose four points $P_1, P_2, P_3, P_4$ independently and uniformly at random on the surface of the unit sphere $S^2 \subset \mathbb{R}^3$.

**What is the probability that the tetrahedron $P_1P_2P_3P_4$ contains the center of the sphere?**

*Warm-up (optional):* Three points are chosen independently and uniformly on a circle. What is the probability that the triangle they form contains the center?

*Hint:* Do not integrate over spherical triangles. A uniform random point on the sphere can be produced in two stages: first pick a random line through the center, then pick one of its two endpoints with a fair coin flip.
