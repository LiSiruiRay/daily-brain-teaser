# The Checkerboard Walk: Can the Knight Return Home?

A standard $8 \times 8$ chessboard is colored in the usual alternating black-and-white pattern. A knight starts on a corner square.

**(a)** Can a knight make a sequence of legal knight moves, visiting every square of the board exactly once, and return to its starting square? (A **closed knight's tour**)

**(b)** Now suppose we remove two squares of the same color. Can a knight's tour (not necessarily closed) exist on the remaining 62 squares?

Give a proof or disproof for each part, explaining the key idea.
