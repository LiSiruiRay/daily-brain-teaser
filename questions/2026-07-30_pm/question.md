# The Conformal Map That Squares the Disk

Let $f: \mathbb{D} \to \mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic on the open unit disk $\mathbb{D} = \{|z| < 1\}$, continuous on $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$, with $f(0) = 0$.

Suppose $|f(z)| \leq |z|^2$ for all $z$ on the unit circle $|z| = 1$.

**Prove that** $|f(z)| \leq |z|^2$ for **all** $z \in \mathbb{D}$.

Then determine: what are all functions $f$ achieving equality $|f(z_0)| = |z_0|^2$ for some $z_0 \neq 0$ inside the disk?
