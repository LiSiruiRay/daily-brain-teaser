# The Integral That Knows Its Arctangent Family

Evaluate the definite integral

$$I = \int_0^1 \frac{\ln(1+x)}{1+x^2}\,dx.$$

Wait — you've seen that one before! Instead, evaluate the **cousin**:

$$I = \int_0^\infty \frac{\arctan(x)}{1+x^2}\,dx.$$
