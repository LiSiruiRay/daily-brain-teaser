# The Fundamental Group of the Punctured Plane

Let $X = \mathbb{R}^2 \setminus \{p_1, p_2\}$ be the plane with **two** points removed.

(a) What is $\pi_1(X)$?

(b) Is $\pi_1(X)$ abelian?

(c) Bonus: How does this generalize to $\mathbb{R}^2$ with $n$ points removed?
