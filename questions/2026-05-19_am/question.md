# The Baire Category Theorem: A Dense $G_\delta$ That Is Meager?

Consider the metric space $\mathbb{R}$ with the standard topology.

The rationals $\mathbb{Q}$ are **dense** in $\mathbb{R}$.

**Question:** Can a complete metric space $X$ be written as a countable union of **nowhere dense** closed sets? In particular, can $\mathbb{R} = \bigcup_{n=1}^\infty F_n$ where each $F_n$ is closed and nowhere dense?

Use this to prove: **$\mathbb{Q}$ is not a $G_\delta$ set** (i.e., $\mathbb{Q}$ cannot be written as a countable intersection of open sets in $\mathbb{R}$).
