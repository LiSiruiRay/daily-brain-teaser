# The Quotient Space That Becomes a Familiar Surface

Let $D^2 = \{(x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x^2 + y^2 \leq 1\}$ be the closed unit disk, and let $S^1 = \partial D^2$ be its boundary circle.

Define an equivalence relation $\sim$ on $D^2$ by:
$$x \sim y \iff x = y \quad \text{or} \quad x, y \in S^1.$$

In other words, we collapse the entire boundary circle to a single point.

**What familiar topological space is $D^2 / S^1$ homeomorphic to? Prove it.**
