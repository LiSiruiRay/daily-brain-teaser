# The Wedge That Knows Its Loops

Let $X = \mathbb{R}P^2 \vee S^1$ (the wedge of the real projective plane with the circle).

Compute $\pi_1(X)$ and determine: does $X$ have a connected 2-sheeted covering space? If so, describe it; if not, explain why.
