# The Residue at Infinity

Let $f(z) = \dfrac{z^3}{z^2 + 1}$.

(a) Compute all finite residues of $f$ (at poles in $\mathbb{C}$).

(b) Show that the sum of **all** residues of a rational function — including the **residue at infinity** — equals zero.

(c) Use this to compute the residue of $f$ at $\infty$ without any Laurent expansion around $\infty$.

> **Recall:** The residue at infinity is defined as
> $$\operatorname{Res}_{z=\infty} f(z) = -\operatorname{Res}_{z=0}\left[\frac{1}{z^2} f\!\left(\frac{1}{z}\right)\right].$$
