# The Circle Has Only One Entire Parametrization

Find **all** pairs of entire functions $f, g : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ such that

$$
f(z)^2 + g(z)^2 = 1 \qquad \text{for all } z \in \mathbb{C}.
$$

The pair $f = \cos z$, $g = \sin z$ is one solution, and so is $f=\cos(z^3+e^z)$, $g=\sin(z^3+e^z)$. Are there any others? For example, is there a solution that is not of the form $(\cos h, \sin h)$, or one built from entirely different functions?

*Follow-up (optional):* What happens for $f^n + g^n = 1$ with $n \ge 3$?
