# The Fundamental Group of RP²

Compute the fundamental group $\pi_1(\mathbb{RP}^2)$.

Recall that the real projective plane $\mathbb{RP}^2$ can be constructed as the quotient of $S^2$ by the antipodal map: identifying each point $x \in S^2$ with $-x$. Equivalently, it can be viewed as a disk $D^2$ with antipodal boundary points identified.

Use the disk model and van Kampen's theorem to compute $\pi_1(\mathbb{RP}^2)$.
