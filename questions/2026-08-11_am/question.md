# The Torus Minus a Point Deformation Retracts onto a Wedge

Let $T = S^1 \times S^1$ be the torus. Show that $T \setminus \{p\}$ (the torus with one point removed) is homotopy equivalent to $S^1 \vee S^1$.

*Hint: Think about the standard CW structure on the torus.*
