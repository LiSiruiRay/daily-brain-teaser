# Can $A^2 + B^2$ Be Invertible?

Let $A$ and $B$ be different $n \times n$ matrices with real entries. Suppose that

$$A^3 = B^3 \quad \text{and} \quad A^2B = B^2A.$$

Can $A^2 + B^2$ be invertible?
