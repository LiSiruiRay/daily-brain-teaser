# The Fundamental Group of the Projective Plane Minus a Point

Consider the real projective plane $\mathbb{RP}^2$. Remove a single point from it to obtain the punctured projective plane $X = \mathbb{RP}^2 \setminus \{p\}$.

**Compute $\pi_1(X)$.**

*Hint: Think about what $\mathbb{RP}^2$ looks like as a quotient of a disk, and what happens when you remove an interior point.*
