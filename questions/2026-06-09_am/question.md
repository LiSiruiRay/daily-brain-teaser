# The Topologist's Group: Fundamental Group of the Wedge Sum $S^1 \vee S^2$

Compute the fundamental group $\pi_1(S^1 \vee S^2, x_0)$, where $x_0$ is the wedge point.

Is it $\mathbb{Z}$? Is it larger? Does $S^2$ contribute anything?
