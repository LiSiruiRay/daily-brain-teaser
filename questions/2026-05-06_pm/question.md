# The Gambler's Ruin: A Fair Game with a Surprising Exit

A gambler starts with $\$k$ and plays a fair coin-flip game: win $\$1$ with probability $\frac{1}{2}$, lose $\$1$ with probability $\frac{1}{2}$. The game ends when the gambler either reaches $\$N$ (success) or goes broke (ruin).

**(a)** What is the probability the gambler reaches $\$N$ before going broke?

**(b)** Here is the surprising part: what is the **expected number of steps** until the game ends?

The answer to (b) might feel impossible at first — can you guess it before computing?
