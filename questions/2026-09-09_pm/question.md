# The Ballot Box Surprise: Ties in Matching Pennies

Players A and B flip a fair coin $N = 2n$ times. They tally their gains and losses: A wins a point on heads, B wins a point on tails. After the first flip, what is the probability that **at no point during the remaining $2n-1$ flips** does the score return to a tie (0-0)?

For concreteness, compute this for $N = 4$ ($n = 2$), then state the general formula.
