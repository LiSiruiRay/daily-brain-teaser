# The Gambler's Ruin: Who Runs Out First?

Two gamblers, Alice and Bob, sit down to play a series of fair coin flips. At each flip, the loser pays the winner $1. Alice starts with $a$ dollars and Bob starts with $b$ dollars. They play until one of them goes broke.

**What is the probability that Alice wins (i.e., Bob goes broke first)?**

Now for the surprising part: suppose $a = 1$ and $b = 99$. Alice starts nearly broke, facing a casino with deep pockets.
