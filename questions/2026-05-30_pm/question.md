# The Fresnel-Flavored Integral

Compute the definite integral

$$I = \int_0^\infty \frac{\sin x}{\sqrt{x}}\, dx.$$

(You may use the known Gaussian integral $\int_0^\infty e^{-t^2}\,dt = \dfrac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}$.)
