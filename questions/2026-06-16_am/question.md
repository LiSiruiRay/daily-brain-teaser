# The Dunce Hat Is Contractible

Consider the **dunce hat**: take a triangle (a 2-simplex) and identify its three edges according to the word $aaa^{-1}$ — that is, all three edges are identified together, but two of them in one direction and one in the opposite direction.

Is the dunce hat contractible? What is its fundamental group?

*(Hint: think about what "contractible" means for a CW-complex, and whether any familiar theorem might help you decide.)*
