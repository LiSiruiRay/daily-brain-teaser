# The Closed Subgroup of ℝ

Prove that every **closed** subgroup $H$ of $(\mathbb{R}, +)$ is either all of $\mathbb{R}$ or of the form $a\mathbb{Z} = \{na : n \in \mathbb{Z}\}$ for some $a \geq 0$.

In other words: the only closed subgroups of $\mathbb{R}$ are $\{0\}$, $\mathbb{R}$ itself, and the discrete lattices $a\mathbb{Z}$.
