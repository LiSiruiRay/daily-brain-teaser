# A Determinant of Sums

Let $A$ be the $n \times n$ matrix whose $(i,j)$ entry is $\min(i,j)$. That is,

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 2 & \cdots & 2 \\ 1 & 2 & 3 & \cdots & 3 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 1 & 2 & 3 & \cdots & n \end{pmatrix}.$$

Compute $\det(A)$.
