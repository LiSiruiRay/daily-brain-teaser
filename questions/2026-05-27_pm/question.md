# The Collector's Last Coupon — A Waiting Time Surprise

You have a fair, well-shuffled deck of $2n$ cards: exactly $n$ **red** cards and $n$ **black** cards. You flip cards one at a time until you have seen **at least one card of each color**.

**Question:** What is the expected number of cards you must flip?

*(For concreteness, try $n = 1$ and $n = 2$ first to build intuition.)*
