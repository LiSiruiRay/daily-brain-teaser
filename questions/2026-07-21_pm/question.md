# The Quotient of a Torus by an Involution

Let $T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$ be the torus. Define the **antipodal map on the first factor**:

$$\varphi: T^2 \to T^2, \qquad \varphi(z, w) = (-z, w),$$

where we regard $S^1 \subset \mathbb{C}$.

This is an involution (i.e., $\varphi^2 = \text{id}$), so it generates a $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$ action on $T^2$.

**What is the quotient space $T^2 / \mathbb{Z}_2$?** Identify it as a familiar topological space, and justify your answer.
