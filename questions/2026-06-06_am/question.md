# The Integral of arctan(x)/x from 0 to 1... Differentiated

Evaluate the definite integral

$$I(\alpha) = \int_0^1 \frac{\arctan(\alpha x)}{x} \, dx$$

and hence compute

$$\int_0^1 \frac{\arctan(x)}{x} \, dx.$$

*(Hint: differentiate under the integral sign.)*
