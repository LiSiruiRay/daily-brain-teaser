# The Continuous Function That Cannot Be Monotone on Any Interval

Let $f : [0,1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function. Suppose that for every $x \in [0,1]$, there exists $y \in [0,1]$ with $y > x$ such that $f(y) > f(x)$.

Must $f$ be non-decreasing on some subinterval $[a,b] \subset [0,1]$ with $a < b$?
