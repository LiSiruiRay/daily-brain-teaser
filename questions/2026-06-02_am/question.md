# The Fundamental Group of the Wedge Sum $S^1 \vee S^1$

Let $X = S^1 \vee S^1$ be the wedge sum of two circles (two circles glued at a single point). What is the fundamental group $\pi_1(X)$?

Now here is the conceptual puzzle: both $S^1$ and $S^1$ have fundamental group $\mathbb{Z}$ (which is *abelian*). Yet $\pi_1(S^1 \vee S^1)$ is **non-abelian**. How can gluing two spaces with abelian fundamental groups produce a non-abelian group?

Compute $\pi_1(S^1 \vee S^1)$ and exhibit a concrete pair of loops that do **not** commute.
