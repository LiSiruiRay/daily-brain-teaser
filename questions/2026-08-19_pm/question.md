# The Three-Cornered Duel

$A$, $B$, and $C$ are to fight a three-cornered pistol duel. All know that $A$'s probability of hitting his target is $1/3$, $B$'s is $2/3$, and $C$ never misses. They fire in the order $A$, $B$, $C$ (cyclically; a player who is hit drops out and is no longer a target). Each player chooses their own target freely on their turn.

**What is $A$'s optimal strategy on his first shot, and what is his resulting probability of survival?**
