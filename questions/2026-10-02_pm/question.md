# The Integers That Sum to Their Product

Find all finite multisets of positive integers greater than 1 whose **sum equals their product**.

In other words, find all ways to choose positive integers $a_1 \leq a_2 \leq \cdots \leq a_n$, each at least 2, such that

$$a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_n = a_1 \cdot a_2 \cdots a_n.$$

List all solutions (there are only a few).
