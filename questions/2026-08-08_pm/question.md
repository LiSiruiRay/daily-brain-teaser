# The Integral That Forgets Its Argument

Evaluate:

$$I = \int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{1}{1 + \tan^{\sqrt{2}}(x)}\, dx.$$

(Here $\tan^{\sqrt{2}}(x) = (\tan x)^{\sqrt{2}}$.)
