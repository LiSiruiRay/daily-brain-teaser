# The Sequence That Must Have a Convergent Subsequence... or Does It?

Let $f_n : [0,1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be a sequence of functions defined by

$$f_n(x) = \sin(nx).$$

Does $(f_n)$ have a subsequence that converges **pointwise almost everywhere** on $[0,1]$?

Surprisingly: **yes**. But can you find a subsequence that converges **pointwise everywhere** on $[0,1]$?

More precisely: prove that **no subsequence** $f_{n_k}(x) = \sin(n_k x)$ converges at every point of $[0,1]$.
