# The Penny-Passing Game: Who Wins?

Players $1, 2, 3, \ldots, n$ are seated around a table, each starting with one penny. The passing rule alternates:

- Player 1 passes **1 penny** to Player 2.
- Player 2 passes **2 pennies** to Player 3.
- Player 3 passes **1 penny** to Player 4.
- Player 4 passes **2 pennies** to Player 5.
- And so on — players alternately pass 1 or 2 pennies to the next player who still has pennies.

A player who runs out of pennies **drops out**. The game ends when one player holds all $n$ pennies.

**Find an infinite set of values of $n$ for which some player ends up with all $n$ pennies.**

*(Putnam 1997, A-2)*
