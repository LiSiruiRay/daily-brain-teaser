# The Holomorphic Function That Cannot Be Analytically Continued

Let $f$ be holomorphic on the open unit disk $\mathbb{D} = \{|z| < 1\}$, defined by

$$f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} z^{n!}.$$

Show that $f$ cannot be analytically continued beyond $\mathbb{D}$; that is, $\mathbb{D}$ is the **natural domain** of $f$ (every point of the unit circle $|z|=1$ is a singularity).

*Hint: Consider what happens at roots of unity.*
