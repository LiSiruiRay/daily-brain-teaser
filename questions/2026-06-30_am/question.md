# The Quotient of a Compact Space by a Closed Equivalence Relation

Let $X = [0, 1]$ and define an equivalence relation $\sim$ on $X$ by identifying all points in the closed set $\{0, 1\}$ to a single point. Let $X/{\sim}$ denote the quotient space.

**Prove that $X/{\sim}$ is homeomorphic to the circle $S^1$.**

More precisely: describe explicitly a homeomorphism $f: X/{\sim} \to S^1$, and explain why the quotient map machinery guarantees it is indeed a homeomorphism — not just a continuous bijection.
