# The Sphere Is Simply Connected: A Covering Space Argument

Let $S^2$ denote the 2-sphere. Prove that $\pi_1(S^2) = 0$ (i.e., $S^2$ is simply connected) using a covering space / lifting argument — **without** van Kampen's theorem or CW-complex cell-by-cell arguments.

*Hint: Think about what a non-trivial element of $\pi_1(S^2)$ would give you.*
