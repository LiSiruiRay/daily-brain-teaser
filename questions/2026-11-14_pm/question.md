# The Square-Root Sandwich

Evaluate
$$
I \;=\; \int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{dx}{\left(\sqrt{\sin x}+\sqrt{\cos x}\right)^4}.
$$

*Hint-free challenge:* the answer is a simple rational number. Can you see why before computing anything?

**Bonus:** For a positive integer $n$, evaluate
$$
I_n \;=\; \int_0^{\pi/2} \frac{dx}{\left(\sin^{1/n} x+\cos^{1/n} x\right)^{2n}}.
$$
