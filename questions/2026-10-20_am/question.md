# A Connected Metric Space Cannot Be Countable

Let $(X,d)$ be a **connected** metric space with at least two points.

**(a)** Prove that $X$ is uncountable.

**(b)** Prove the stronger statement $|X| \ge |\mathbb{R}|$, i.e. $X$ has at least the cardinality of the continuum.

**(c)** (Discussion, no proof required.) Does the conclusion of (a) still hold if "metric" is weakened to "Hausdorff"?

*Hint-free challenge:* try to find a proof that is only a few lines long.
