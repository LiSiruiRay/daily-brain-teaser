# The Stone–Weierstrass Shortcut: When Does a Subalgebra Separate Points?

Let $A$ be the set of all continuous functions $f : [0,1] \to \mathbb{R}$ of the form

$$f(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{N} a_k x^{2k}$$

for some $N \geq 0$ and real coefficients $a_k$. That is, $A$ is the algebra of all polynomials in $x^2$.

**Question:** Is $A$ dense in $C([0,1])$ (with the sup-norm)?

Now consider instead

$$B = \left\{ f \in C([0,1]) : f(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{N} a_k x^{2k+1} \right\}$$

the algebra generated by $x$ (all polynomials in $x$ with only **odd** powers, plus the zero function).

**Which of $A$ and $B$ is dense in $C([0,1])$, and which is not? Identify the precise obstruction for the one that fails.**
