# The Maximum Modulus Principle: Where Can a Holomorphic Function Achieve Its Maximum?

Let $f$ be a holomorphic function on the open unit disk $\mathbb{D} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| < 1\}$, continuous on $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$.

Suppose $|f(z)| \leq 1$ for all $z \in \partial \mathbb{D}$ (the boundary), and that $|f(z_0)| = 1$ for some interior point $z_0 \in \mathbb{D}$.

**What can you conclude about $f$?**

Now push further: suppose instead $f : \mathbb{D} \to \mathbb{D}$ is holomorphic, $f(0) = 0$, and $|f(z_0)| = |z_0|$ for some $z_0 \neq 0$.

Again, what can you conclude?
