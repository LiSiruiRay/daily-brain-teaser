# Two Integrals, One Curve

**(a)** Evaluate
$$I=\int_0^1 \left( \sqrt[3]{1-x^7}\;-\;\sqrt[7]{1-x^3}\right)\,dx .$$

**(b)** Let $f(x)=x^5+x$. This is a strictly increasing bijection $\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$, but $f^{-1}$ has no elementary closed form. Evaluate
$$J=\int_0^2 f^{-1}(y)\,dy .$$

*(Try to avoid Beta/Gamma functions in (a) and avoid solving a quintic in (b).)*
