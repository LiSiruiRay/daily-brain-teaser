# The Compact Space That Cannot Be Hausdorff

Let $X$ be an infinite set equipped with the **cofinite topology**: a set $U \subseteq X$ is open if and only if $U = \emptyset$ or $X \setminus U$ is finite.

**(a)** Show that $X$ is compact.

**(b)** Show that $X$ is **not** Hausdorff (when $|X|$ is infinite).

**(c)** Now let $f: X \to Y$ be a continuous bijection from $(X, \mathcal{T}_{\text{cofinite}})$ to a Hausdorff space $Y$. What can you conclude about $f$, and why?
