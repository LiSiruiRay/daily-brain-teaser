# The Continuous Function That Must Change Sign

Let $f : [0,1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous, with
$$\int_0^1 f(x)\, dx = 0 \quad \text{and} \quad \int_0^1 x f(x)\, dx = 0.$$

Must $f$ have **at least two** sign changes on $(0,1)$? (A "sign change" means a point where $f$ transitions from strictly positive to strictly negative or vice versa.)

Prove your answer.
