# Curing the Compulsive Gambler

Mr. Brown always bets \$1 on the number 13 at roulette. An American wheel has 38 equally likely numbers. If his number comes up, he is paid 35 times his stake and keeps his original stake. Otherwise he loses the \$1.

Kind Friend wants to cure Brown of gambling. So Friend makes a side bet: **\$20 at even money that Brown will be behind (strictly losing) at the end of 36 plays.**

**(a)** Account for both the casino and the side bet. What is Brown's expected net gain over 36 plays? How is the cure working?

**(b)** Kind Friend realizes something has gone wrong. He keeps the same \$20 even-money bet that Brown is behind, but changes the horizon from 36 plays to **37 plays**. What happens now?

*(No heavy computation is needed. You may use $(37/38)^{36} \approx 0.383$.)*
