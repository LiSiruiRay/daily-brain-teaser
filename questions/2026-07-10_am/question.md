# A Polynomial Evaluated at Consecutive Integers

Let $p(x)$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients. Suppose that $p(1), p(2), p(3), \ldots, p(2025)$ are all odd. Must $p$ have at least one integer root? What can you say about the number of integer roots of $p$?

More precisely: **prove that $p$ has no integer roots at all.**
