# A Finite Set Closed Under Cross Product

Let $\times$ denote the cross product in $\mathbb{R}^3$. For which positive integers $n$ does there exist a set $S\subset\mathbb{R}^3$ with **exactly $n$ elements** such that

$$S=\{\,v\times w \;:\; v,w\in S\,\}\ ?$$

Note that this is an equality of sets. Every cross product of two elements of $S$ must lie in $S$, and every element of $S$ must arise as such a cross product.

*Hint:* the answer is not "only $n=1$."
