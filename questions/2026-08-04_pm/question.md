# The Torus and the Annulus: A Quotient Surprise

Consider the torus $T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$. Define an equivalence relation $\sim$ on $T^2$ by

$$(e^{i\theta_1}, e^{i\theta_2}) \sim (e^{i\theta_1}, e^{-i\theta_2})$$

(reflecting the second coordinate), with all other points equivalent only to themselves.

**What is the quotient space $T^2 / {\sim}$?**

Identify it as a familiar topological space. Can you prove your answer?

*Hint: Think about what happens to each "fiber" $\{e^{i\theta_1}\} \times S^1$ under the equivalence relation.*
