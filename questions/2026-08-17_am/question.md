# The Integral That Knows Γ

Compute the integral
$$I = \int_0^\infty x^{s-1} e^{-x} \ln x \, dx, \quad s > 0.$$

Express your answer in terms of $\Gamma(s)$, $\Gamma'(s)$, and/or the digamma function $\psi(s) = \Gamma'(s)/\Gamma(s)$.
