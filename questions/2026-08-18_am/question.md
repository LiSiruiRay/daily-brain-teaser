# The Closed Disk Minus a Boundary Arc: What's the Fundamental Group?

Let $D^2 = \{(x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : x^2 + y^2 \leq 1\}$ be the closed unit disk, and let $A \subset S^1 = \partial D^2$ be a proper closed arc (say, the upper semicircle $A = \{(\cos\theta, \sin\theta) : 0 \leq \theta \leq \pi\}$).

Consider the space $X = D^2 \setminus A$ (the closed disk with the upper boundary arc removed).

**Question:** Is $X$ simply connected? Compute $\pi_1(X)$ and justify your answer.
