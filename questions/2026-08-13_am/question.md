# The Holomorphic Map That Fixes Too Many Points

Let $f: \mathbb{D} \to \mathbb{D}$ be a holomorphic map from the open unit disk to itself. Suppose $f$ has **two distinct fixed points** $z_1, z_2 \in \mathbb{D}$ (i.e., $f(z_1) = z_1$ and $f(z_2) = z_2$).

Prove that $f$ must be the identity map.
