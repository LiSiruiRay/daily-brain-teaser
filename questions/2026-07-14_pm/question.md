# The Topological Group That Must Be Discrete or Dense

Let $G$ be a topological group and $H \leq G$ a subgroup. Show that if $H$ is **not** dense in $G$, then the closure $\overline{H}$ is a **proper** closed subgroup of $G$ — and conclude that any subgroup of a topological group is either **dense** or its closure is still a proper subgroup. Then use this to prove:

> **Any open subgroup of a topological group is also closed.**
