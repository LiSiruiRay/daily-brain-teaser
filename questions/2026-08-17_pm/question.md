# The Integral That Refuses to Converge Absolutely

Define
$$I = \int_0^\infty \frac{\sin x}{x}\,dx.$$

(a) Show that $I$ converges (as an improper Riemann integral).

(b) Show that $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty \frac{|\sin x|}{x}\,dx = +\infty$, so the integral does **not** converge absolutely.

(c) Evaluate $I$.
