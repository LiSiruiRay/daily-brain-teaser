# A Polynomial Vanishing on All Lattice Points in a Strip

Let $p(x, y)$ be a polynomial with real coefficients such that $p(m, n) = 0$ for every pair of integers $(m, n)$ with $m \geq 0$ and $0 \leq n \leq m$. Must $p$ be identically zero?
