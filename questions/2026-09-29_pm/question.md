# The Quotient of a Disk by Its Boundary Reflection

Let $D^2 = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| \leq 1\}$ be the closed unit disk, and define an equivalence relation $\sim$ on $D^2$ by:

$$z \sim w \iff z = w \quad \text{or} \quad (|z| = |w| = 1 \text{ and } w = \bar{z})$$

That is, we identify each boundary point $e^{i\theta}$ with its complex conjugate $e^{-i\theta}$.

**What is the quotient space $D^2/{\sim}$, up to homeomorphism?**

*Hint: Think about what the boundary circle becomes after the identification.*
