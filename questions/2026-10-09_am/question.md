# The Polynomial That Stays Small on Integers

Let $p(x)$ be a polynomial of degree $n$ with leading coefficient $1$ (i.e., monic). Prove that

$$\max_{0 \le x \le n} |p(x)| \ge \frac{n!}{2^n}.$$

In other words, no monic degree-$n$ polynomial can be uniformly small on the integers $0, 1, \ldots, n$.
