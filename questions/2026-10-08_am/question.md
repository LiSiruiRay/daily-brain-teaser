# The Holomorphic Function That Must Vanish on an Arc

Let $f$ be a holomorphic function on the open unit disk $\mathbb{D}$, continuous on the closed disk $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$. Suppose that $f(z) = 0$ for all $z$ on the arc

$$A = \{ e^{i\theta} : \theta \in [0, \pi] \}$$

(the upper semicircle of $\partial \mathbb{D}$).

Must $f$ be identically zero on $\mathbb{D}$?
