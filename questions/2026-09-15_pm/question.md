# The Torus Knot Group: When Two Loops Cannot Be Separated

Let $T = S^1 \times S^1$ be the torus. Consider the two standard loops:
- $a$: the meridian (goes around the "small" circle once)
- $b$: the longitude (goes around the "large" circle once)

We know $\pi_1(T) \cong \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$, generated by $[a]$ and $[b]$.

Now consider the loop $\gamma$ on the torus that wraps **$p$ times** in the $a$-direction and **$q$ times** in the $b$-direction, where $\gcd(p,q) = 1$.

**Question:** Show that $\gamma$ is a **simple closed curve** on $T$ (it doesn't self-intersect), and that cutting the torus along $\gamma$ produces an **annulus** (a cylinder), not two separate pieces.

As a follow-up: what is the fundamental group of the **complement** $T \setminus \gamma$?
