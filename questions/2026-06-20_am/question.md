# The Beta-Flavored Integral

Evaluate the definite integral

$$I = \int_0^1 x^3 (1-x)^4 \, dx.$$

Then use your answer to find a closed form for

$$J = \int_0^1 x^3 (1-x)^4 (1 + 10x) \, dx.$$
