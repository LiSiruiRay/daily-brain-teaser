# The Ballot Problem's Cousin: Runs of Heads

You flip a fair coin $n$ times. A **run of heads** is a maximal consecutive sequence of heads. For example, in the sequence HHTHHH, there are two runs of heads (of lengths 2 and 3).

**Question:** What is the expected number of runs of heads in $n$ fair coin flips?
