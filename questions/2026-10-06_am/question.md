# The Torus Minus a Disk Is a Punctured Torus... Or Is It?

Let $T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$ be the torus, and let $D \subset T^2$ be a closed disk embedded in the torus. Consider the space $X = T^2 \setminus \text{int}(D)$, the torus with an open disk removed.

**Compute $\pi_1(X)$ and describe $X$ up to homotopy type.**

As a hint to get you started: think about the standard CW-structure on $T^2$ with one 0-cell, two 1-cells, and one 2-cell.
