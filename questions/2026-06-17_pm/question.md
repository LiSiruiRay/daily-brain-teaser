# The Absent-Minded Secretary

A secretary types $n$ letters and $n$ envelopes, then stuffs the letters into envelopes completely at random (one letter per envelope). What is the probability that **no** letter ends up in the correct envelope?

Now here is the surprising part: what does this probability approach as $n \to \infty$?
