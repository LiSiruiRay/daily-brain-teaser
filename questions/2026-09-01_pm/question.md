# The Covering Space That Wraps Around Twice

Let $p: S^1 \to S^1$ be the map $p(z) = z^2$ (viewing $S^1 \subset \mathbb{C}$).

**(a)** Show directly that $p$ is a covering map, and identify the number of sheets.

**(b)** The induced homomorphism $p_*: \pi_1(S^1, 1) \to \pi_1(S^1, 1)$ is a map $\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$. What is it explicitly?

**(c)** Now consider the induced map on fundamental groups for the $n$-sheeted cover $p_n(z) = z^n$. Without further calculation, what does this tell you about which subgroups of $\mathbb{Z}$ arise as images of covering-induced maps $\pi_1(S^1) \to \pi_1(S^1)$?
