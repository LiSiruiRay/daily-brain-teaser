# The Polynomial Pigeonhole

Let $p(x)$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients. Suppose $p(a) = p(b) = p(c) = 7$ for three distinct integers $a, b, c$. Prove that there is no integer $d$ such that $p(d) = 14$.
