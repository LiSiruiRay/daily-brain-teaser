# The Space That Is $T_1$ but Not Hausdorff — and Its Compact Subsets

Consider $\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ (the positive integers) equipped with the **cofinite topology**: a set $U \subseteq \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ is open if $U = \emptyset$ or $\mathbb{Z}_{>0} \setminus U$ is finite.

**(a)** Show that this space is $T_1$ (points are closed) but **not** Hausdorff.

**(b)** Show that **every** subset of $\mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ (with the subspace topology) is compact.

**(c)** Conclude that in a non-Hausdorff space, compact subsets need not be closed.
