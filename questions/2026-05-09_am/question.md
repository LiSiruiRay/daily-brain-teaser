# The Gaussian Integral's Elegant Cousin

Evaluate the definite integral:

$$I = \int_0^\infty \frac{\sin x}{\sqrt{x}} \, dx$$

You may use the fact that $\displaystyle\int_0^\infty e^{-t^2} dt = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}$.
