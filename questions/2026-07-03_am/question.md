# The Polynomial That Is Always Divisible by n!

Let $n$ be a positive integer. Show that for any integers $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n$, the product

$$\prod_{1 \le i < j \le n} (a_j - a_i)$$

is divisible by $1! \cdot 2! \cdots (n-1)!$.
