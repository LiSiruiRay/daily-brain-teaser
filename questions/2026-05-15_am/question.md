# A Polynomial That Takes the Value 1 Too Often

Let $p(x)$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients. Suppose that $p(a_1) = p(a_2) = p(a_3) = p(a_4) = 1$ for four distinct integers $a_1, a_2, a_3, a_4$. Prove that there is no integer $b$ with $p(b) = -1$.
