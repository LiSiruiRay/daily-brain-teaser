# Sum of Squares Under a Linear Constraint

Let $A$ be a positive real number. What are the possible values of
$$\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} x_j^2,$$
given that $x_0, x_1, x_2, \ldots$ are **positive** real numbers satisfying
$$\sum_{j=0}^{\infty} x_j = A?$$
