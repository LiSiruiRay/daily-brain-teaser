# The Gambler's Fair Coin Sequence

You repeatedly flip a fair coin. What is the expected number of flips until the pattern **HH** first appears? What about until **HT** first appears?

Both patterns require seeing two specific consecutive outcomes — yet their expected waiting times are **different**. Find both expected values and explain intuitively why they differ.
