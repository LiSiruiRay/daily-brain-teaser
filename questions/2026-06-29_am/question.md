# The Weierstrass M-Test Is Tight: A Conditionally Convergent Series of Functions

Consider the series
$$f(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^n}{n} \sin(nx), \quad x \in \mathbb{R}.$$

(a) Show that for each fixed $x \in (0, 2\pi)$, the series converges.

(b) Show that the convergence is **not** uniform on $(0, 2\pi)$.

(Hint for (b): Think about what happens near $x = 0$.)
