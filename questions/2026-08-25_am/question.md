# The Torus That Forgets a Disk

Let $T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$ be the torus. Remove an open disk $D$ from $T^2$ to obtain the **punctured torus** $T^2 \setminus D$.

Show that $T^2 \setminus D$ deformation retracts onto a wedge of two circles $S^1 \vee S^1$, and conclude that

$$\pi_1(T^2 \setminus D) \cong F_2,$$

the **free group on two generators**.

As a bonus, use this to understand geometrically why $\pi_1(T^2) \cong \mathbb{Z}^2$ is the *abelianization* of $F_2$.
