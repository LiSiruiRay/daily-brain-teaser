# The Holomorphic Function Determined by Its Real Part

Let $f = u + iv$ be a holomorphic function on a connected open domain $\Omega \subseteq \mathbb{C}$. Suppose that the real part $u(x, y)$ is identically zero on $\Omega$.

**Prove that $f$ is constant on $\Omega$.**

Now suppose instead that $|f(z)|$ is constant on $\Omega$. Must $f$ be constant?
