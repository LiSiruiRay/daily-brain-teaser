# Twelve Lengths and an Acute Triangle

Let $d_1, d_2, \dots, d_{12}$ be real numbers in the **open** interval $(1, 12)$.

Show that there exist distinct indices $i, j, k$ such that $d_i, d_j, d_k$ are the side lengths of an **acute** triangle.

*Follow-up:* Why is the number $12$, appearing twice, not a coincidence? What goes wrong if the interval is the closed interval $[1,12]$?
