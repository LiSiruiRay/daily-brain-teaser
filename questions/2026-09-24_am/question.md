# The Holomorphic Function That Integrates to Zero on Every Circle

Let $f$ be a continuous function on $\mathbb{C}$. Suppose that for **every** circle $C$ in the plane (of any center and any radius),

$$\oint_C f(z)\, dz = 0.$$

Must $f$ be holomorphic?
