# The Equicontinuous Sequence That Must Converge

Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of functions on $[0,1]$ that is **equicontinuous** and **pointwise bounded** (i.e., $\sup_n |f_n(x)| < \infty$ for each $x \in [0,1]$).

Suppose that $f_n(x) \to f(x)$ for every $x$ in a **dense** subset $D \subseteq [0,1]$.

**Prove that $f_n \to f$ uniformly on $[0,1]$.**
