# The Fundamental Group of the Real Projective Plane: An Unexpected Finite Group

Let $\mathbb{RP}^2$ denote the real projective plane, obtained from $S^2$ by identifying antipodal points: $x \sim -x$.

**(a)** Show that the quotient map $q: S^2 \to \mathbb{RP}^2$ is a 2-sheeted covering space.

**(b)** Using covering space theory, determine $\pi_1(\mathbb{RP}^2, [p])$.

**(c)** Why is it surprising that a non-trivial loop in $\mathbb{RP}^2$ traversed **twice** becomes null-homotopic, even though traversed once it is not?
