# Determinant Tic-Tac-Toe

Player 1 and Player 0 alternately fill in a $3 \times 3$ matrix, with Player 1 writing **1**'s and Player 0 writing **0**'s. Player 1 goes first, so the final matrix has five 1's and four 0's.

**Player 0 wins** if the determinant is 0. **Player 1 wins** if the determinant is nonzero.

Assuming both players play optimally, **who wins?**
