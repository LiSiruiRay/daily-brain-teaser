# The Collector's Rival: Who Finishes First?

Two players, Alice and Bob, each independently collect coupons. Each day, each player receives one coupon chosen uniformly at random from $n$ types (independently of each other and of previous days).

Alice needs to collect **all $n$ types** to win. Bob only needs to collect **2 specific types** (say types 1 and 2) to win.

For $n = 4$, who is more likely to finish first?

*(Hint: think carefully about what "finishing" means for each player before computing.)*
