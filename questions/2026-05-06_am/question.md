# The Collector's Coupon Problem

There are $n$ distinct types of coupons. Each time you buy a cereal box, you receive one coupon chosen uniformly at random from the $n$ types (independently each time).

**Question:** What is the **expected number of boxes** you must buy until you have collected **at least one coupon of every type**?

For concreteness, compute the answer for $n = 6$ (like rolling a fair die until every face has appeared).

Then show that for large $n$, the expected number of boxes is approximately $n \ln n$.
