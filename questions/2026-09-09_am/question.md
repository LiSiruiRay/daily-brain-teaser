# The Lazy Gambler's Last Dollar

A gambler walks into a casino with $\$3$. At each round, he bets $\$1$: with probability $p = 2/3$ he wins $\$1$, and with probability $q = 1/3$ he loses $\$1$. The casino has only $\$3$ (so the total money in play is $\$6$). They play until one side is broke.

1. What is the probability that the gambler wins all $\$6$?
2. What is the **expected number of rounds** until the game ends?
