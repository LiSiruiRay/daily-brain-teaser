# The Möbius Band Has Fundamental Group ℤ

Consider the **Möbius band** $M$, constructed by taking the square $[0,1] \times [0,1]$ and identifying $(0, t) \sim (1, 1-t)$ for all $t \in [0,1]$.

**Show that $\pi_1(M) \cong \mathbb{Z}$**, and identify a geometric generator.

As a follow-up to think about: the boundary of $M$ is a single circle. When you go around this boundary circle once, how many times does the generator of $\pi_1(M)$ get traversed?
