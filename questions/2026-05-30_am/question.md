# The Integral of $x^x$'s Cousin

Evaluate the definite integral

$$I = \int_0^1 x \ln x \, dx.$$

Then use the same idea to compute

$$J = \int_0^1 x^n \ln x \, dx \quad \text{for any integer } n \geq 0,$$

and finally evaluate the beautiful series

$$S = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \int_0^1 x^n \ln x \, dx.$$

What does $S$ equal, and why is the answer satisfying?
