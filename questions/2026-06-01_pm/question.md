# The Integral That Knows Its Own Derivative

Let $f: [0,1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be continuous. Suppose that

$$\int_0^1 f(x)\, dx = \int_0^1 x\, f(x)\, dx = 0.$$

Must $f$ have **at least two zeros** in $(0,1)$?

Prove it or find a counterexample.
