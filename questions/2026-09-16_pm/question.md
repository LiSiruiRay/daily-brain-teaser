# The Prisoner's Pardon: Three Boxes, One Clue

Three prisoners — Alice, Bob, and Carol — are told that exactly two of them will be pardoned tomorrow, but none knows who. Alice asks the guard to privately tell her the name of **one** prisoner other than herself who will be pardoned. The guard (who knows the truth) whispers "Bob."

**Question:** After hearing "Bob," what is Alice's probability of being pardoned?

Many people instinctively say her probability should *increase* from $2/3$ to something higher, since she now "knows" something. Others say it drops. What is the correct answer, and why?
