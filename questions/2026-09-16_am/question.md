# The Ballot Duplication Trick: Expected Number of Matches

A deck of 52 cards is shuffled and laid face-up in a row. A second, independently shuffled deck is laid face-up beneath the first — one card below each card of the first deck. 

**What is the expected number of positions where the two cards match (same rank and suit)?**

Now here is the twist: suppose instead of 52 cards, you use a deck of $n$ cards. Does the expected number of matches depend on $n$?
